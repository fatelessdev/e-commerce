## Design Context

### Users & Context
- **Target audience**: Fashion-conscious shoppers in India (Gen-Z and young millennials) who want premium-feel streetwear without a luxury-store price barrier.
- **Usage context**: Mobile-first browsing, fast category filtering, visual product comparison, and quick checkout.
- **Primary job to be done**: Discover high-confidence apparel choices (fit, style, quality signal) and complete purchase while feeling they unlocked a meaningful deal.
- **Core emotional goals**:
  - Baseline: modern luxury, calm confidence, editorial clarity.
  - Peak moments: excitement and delight during AI bargain negotiation and checkout conversion.
- **Trust signals that matter**: Clear pricing hierarchy, restrained use of discount visuals, polished motion, and consistent typography.

### Brand Personality
- **Premium**: Editorial, composed, and materially intentional.
- **Modern**: Clean structure, high-contrast typography, and subtle systemized motion.
- **Engaging**: AI-powered interaction and playful conversion moments without becoming noisy.
- **Reference feel**: Between premium editorial commerce and AI-native product interaction.
- **Anti-reference**: Do not look like a flash-sale marketplace (no loud neon palettes, cluttered badges, or constant discount shouting).

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Minimal, editorial, image-first. Product imagery should carry visual weight while UI chrome stays restrained.

#### Color System (Current Tokens)
- **Light mode foundation**:
  - Background: `oklch(0.985 0.003 60)`
  - Foreground: `oklch(0.14 0.012 30)`
  - Border/Input: `oklch(0.905 0.006 50)`
- **Dark mode foundation**:
  - Background: `oklch(0.115 0.01 30)`
  - Foreground: `oklch(0.93 0.006 60)`
  - Border/Input: translucent neutral tokens with low visual noise.
- **Primary accent**:
  - Red accent token: `--red` (deep, desaturated crimson)
  - Use only for intentional moments: primary conversion CTA, AI bargain prompts, active high-priority states.
  - Never flood pages with red; treat it as a directional signal.
- **Surface strategy**:
  - Prefer solid surfaces over glass effects.
  - Use muted neutrals for scaffolding, not decoration.
  - Optional grain texture overlay is acceptable at very low opacity to create a premium tactile finish.

#### Typography System
- **Font family**: Outfit (weights 300-900) as global sans.
- **Hierarchy style**:
  - Hero and key headlines: uppercase, heavy weight (800-900), tight tracking.
  - Utility labels and microcopy: uppercase with expanded letter spacing (`tracking-[0.1em]` to `0.3em`).
  - Body/supporting text: lighter weight with relaxed line-height.
- **Numeric treatment**: Prefer tabular numerals for prices and counts where comparison matters.

#### Layout and Composition Patterns
- **Section rhythm**: Generous vertical spacing (`py-14` to `py-28`) and consistent horizontal gutters (`px-6`, `md:px-12`, `lg:px-16`).
- **Content width**: Favor centered containers with strong breathing room (commonly max `7xl`).
- **Product cards**: Image-first, `3:4` aspect ratio, minimal framing, subtle hover response.
- **Navigation and toolbars**: Functional, compact, and high-clarity, with sticky behavior where it supports browsing.

#### Imagery and Iconography
- **Imagery**: Full-bleed product photography, minimal overlays, only light darkening for text legibility.
- **Badges**: Sparse and meaningful (e.g., sold out, new).
- **Icons**: Lucide line icons, small sizes, clean silhouettes, never overly decorative.

### Motion and Interaction System
- **Core easing tokens**:
  - `--ease-out-expo: cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`
  - `--ease-spring: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`
- **Motion character**: Smooth, physical, and restrained. Motion should make UI feel tactile, not flashy.
- **Common patterns**:
  - Hover lift and image scale for product cards.
  - Staggered reveal and blur-to-sharp entrance for grid content.
  - Gentle modal entrance for AI bargain and conversion moments.
- **Timing guidance**:
  - Micro interactions: ~300-500ms.
  - Content reveals: ~600-900ms.
  - Avoid abrupt linear motion.
- **Reduced motion support**:
  - Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
  - Disable or simplify bounce/transform-heavy effects where required.

### Component Styling Conventions
- **Button language**:
  - Default buttons use smooth press feedback and refined easing.
  - Critical CTA buttons may use red accent; secondary actions remain neutral/outline.
  - Squared edges (`rounded-none`) are acceptable in editorial and commerce CTAs.
- **Card language**:
  - Minimal border treatment and smooth transitions.
  - In product contexts, avoid boxed card styling in favor of content-first presentation.
- **Inputs and filters**:
  - Compact, crisp controls with clear focus rings.
  - Filter chips and labels use uppercase micro-typography for a retail-system feel.
- **AI bargain surfaces**:
  - Distinct but still premium.
  - Red accent highlights negotiation actions; chat remains readable and calm.

### Accessibility and Inclusion
- **Baseline target**: WCAG 2.2 AA.
- **Contrast**: Maintain strong text/background contrast in both light and dark themes, including muted states.
- **Keyboard and focus**: All interactive controls require visible focus indicators and accessible labels.
- **Motion sensitivity**: Preserve usability when reduced motion is enabled.
- **Content clarity**: Keep labels short, direct, and action-oriented, especially in checkout and bargain flows.

### Design Principles
1. **Content over chrome**: Prioritize product imagery and typography; remove decorative UI noise.
2. **Accent with intent**: Use crimson as a high-signal guide, not a background color strategy.
3. **Editorial precision**: Prefer deliberate spacing, strong alignment, and tight type hierarchy over visual clutter.
4. **Premium motion discipline**: Use spring/expo easing to add tactility while keeping transitions calm and purposeful.
5. **Conversion without cheapness**: Make deal mechanics exciting, but never let the interface feel discount-first.
6. **Accessible by default**: Build for WCAG 2.2 AA with robust contrast, focus visibility, and reduced-motion compatibility.

### Quick Do and Avoid
- **Do**:
  - Keep layouts airy and image-led.
  - Use uppercase micro-labels and tight headline typography for editorial voice.
  - Reserve red accent for CTAs, AI bargain triggers, and active high-priority states.
  - Keep interaction feedback smooth and physically plausible.
- **Avoid**:
  - Flash-sale visuals, aggressive promotional noise, and crowded badge stacks.
  - Heavy glassmorphism or effect-first surfaces.
  - Overusing red in neutral browsing contexts.
  - Dense UI blocks that compete with product photography.
