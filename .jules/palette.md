
## 2024-05-23 - Form Inputs Accessibility
**Learning:** Adding explicit `id` and `htmlFor` bindings to labels and inputs provides screen reader support and increases the click target area, improving form usability for all users. The admin panel forms are a good target for these enhancements.
**Action:** When building or modifying forms, always ensure explicit `<label>` and `<input>` bindings using `htmlFor` and `id`.
