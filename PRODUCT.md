# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

XILAR serves Gen-Z and young millennial streetwear shoppers in India who discover products from mobile-first channels, compare style quickly, and expect checkout to feel trustworthy. The same codebase also supports internal admins managing catalog, inventory, coupons, orders, and merchandising.

## Product Purpose

XILAR is a premium streetwear storefront that sells apparel through SEO-friendly public routes, editorial product presentation, account-backed wishlist flows, Razorpay and COD checkout, store credits, and a checkout bargain AI that can issue short-lived coupons. Success means shoppers can understand the brand instantly, browse fast on mobile, trust prices and stock, and complete checkout without the site feeling like a discount marketplace.

## Brand Personality

Premium, streetwise, and restrained. The interface should feel dark, image-led, confident, and fashion-editorial, with the crimson accent used as a precise interaction signal rather than broad decoration.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic discount marketplace, coupon farm, cluttered ecommerce theme, or template storefront. Avoid constant promotional banners, flashing badges, crowded cards, fake settings surfaces, excessive glassmorphism, and motion that competes with shopping intent.

## Design Principles

- Lead with product presence: product imagery, clean type, and stable first viewports carry the brand more than decorative UI chrome.
- Keep premium motion disciplined: use purposeful transitions, pause offscreen work, respect reduced motion, and avoid stacking heavy effects in one viewport.
- Protect trust at checkout: server-verified pricing, stock, coupon rules, and payment status are more important than client convenience.
- Optimize for real mobile shoppers: public pages should stay cacheable, fast, SEO-safe, and resilient under social/browser webviews.
- Preserve early-stage clarity: fix root causes directly and avoid backward-compatibility shims unless the product explicitly needs them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, meaningful alt text, readable contrast, and reduced-motion alternatives across public and admin UI. Treat color as supportive, not the only way to communicate state, and keep interactive controls usable on mobile touch targets.
